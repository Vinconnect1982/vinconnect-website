About VINCONNECT

Local people. Practical connections.

A family-run installation business connecting homes, rural properties, businesses and communities across South East Victoria.

Built around how you use your property.

A good connection should reach the places where life and work happen. VINCONNECT brings internet, Wi-Fi and cameras together across the home, office, shed, stable and shared spaces.

A hands-on local business

Led by Vince De Stefano, VINCONNECT combines practical installation experience with a focus on clear communication, tidy presentation and helping customers understand their setup.

Based around Cranbourne. Connected to the regions.

Work extends through South East Melbourne, the Mornington Peninsula, Bass Coast and Gippsland.

Practical connections for the places you live, work and look after.
